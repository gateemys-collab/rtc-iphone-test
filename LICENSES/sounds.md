# Sound effects — sources and licences

All sound files in `public/assets/sfx/` are **self-generated by our own synthesiser script** `tools/make-sfx.mjs`
(written for this project, ~90 lines of plain Node: oscillators, noise, envelope, filters → 16-bit WAV; no npm
dependencies, no network). It is **not jsfxr**: no jsfxr (or any other) code, preset or sample was copied or
bundled, and nothing at runtime synthesises audio — the game only plays the 5 pre-rendered WAVs.
No third-party audio samples are included. No background music.

> Note for plan §5 (which says "jsfxr 生成"): the sounds follow the sfxr *parameter model* but are rendered by the
> self-written script above. Licence status: project-owned, no attribution or licence obligations.

| file | what | source | licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| footstep.wav | step on paving (played on walk frames 0 and 3) | generated: low-passed noise burst | project-owned (self-generated, free for commercial use) |
| bubble-pop.wav | interaction bubble appears | generated: rising sine blip | project-owned |
| door-open.wav | home / store door opens | generated: square thunk + noise click + saw tail | project-owned |
| terminal-beep.wav | time terminal opens | generated: two-tone square (arpeggio ×1.5) | project-owned |
| ui-click.wav | UI buttons, panel close, bus stop sign | generated: short square tick | project-owned |

Format: 16-bit mono PCM WAV, 22050 Hz. Regenerate: `node tools/make-sfx.mjs` (deterministic, fixed noise seeds).

## Algorithm credit
The synthesiser parameter model (wave type, attack/sustain/decay envelope, punch, frequency slide and
delta slide, square duty sweep, vibrato, arpeggio, low/high-pass filters) follows **sfxr** by Tomas
Pettersson (DrPetter) and its JavaScript port **jsfxr** (https://github.com/chr15m/jsfxr, released under
the **Unlicense** / public domain). `tools/make-sfx.mjs` is an independent, simplified re-implementation of
that model; no jsfxr code or presets were copied. Credit is recorded voluntarily (the Unlicense does not require it).
